Slot Market Gaps & Framework Trends

A short monthly report for product leads at small and mid slot studios. It answers three questions: what was released, which game frameworks are moving, and where the gaps are.

Data sources

All RTP, volatility, max win, mechanic and release date figures in this report come from the free slot database at slot.report, which tracks more than 6,000 games from over 80 providers and is updated daily. We add release details from provider press pages and public industry news, and every number in the report links to its source.

We use public information only. We do not use paywalled content, scraped review text, or data from any operator or studio's internal systems.

How we classify games

Each release gets one primary framework, based on its published mechanics and features, not its name or theme.

FrameworkTypical signals
Hold and winLocking coins, respins that reset, jackpot tiers
Cluster and cascadeCluster pays, tumbling symbols, large grids
Scatter paysPay anywhere, tumble, multiplier symbols
Ways and MegawaysVariable reel heights, high ways counts
Lines and free spinsPaylines, expanding or sticky symbols in free spins
Persistent stateMeters and upgrades that carry between spins
Multiplier gridCell or spot multipliers that grow
OtherWheel, pick bonus, instant games, or not enough data

Theme and volatility are tracked separately, so a gap can be a mix of all three.

Rules we follow

  • Every market number has a source link next to it.
  • Missing data is marked "No data". We do not estimate figures.
  • Opinions are labelled "Our read:" so facts and views stay apart.
  • A market gap needs a public demand signal and a low recent release count.
  • RTP values are the highest published tier. The RTP at a given casino may be lower.

Limits

This is release and specification data, not revenue data. It shows what studios ship and how games are built. It does not show how well each game performs at operators. Coverage of smaller providers is partial.
